KickSense — Final Year Project Architecture

Bahria University Islamabad · Ashir Ijaz & Ibrahim Tariq · Supervisor: Ms. Zainab Iftikhar

System Architecture

1. Input: 26-frame pose sequence normalized to hip center and torso height scale (132 features/frame).

2. Multi-Scale Inception: Convolutions with kernels 1, 3, and 5 capturing posture, instantaneous velocity, and stride acceleration.

3. Temporal Modeling: Bidirectional LSTM regularized with L2 weight decay (4e-4) and dropout.

4. Shift-Invariance: Dual pooling (GlobalMaxPooling + GlobalAveragePooling) to capture peak strike mechanics regardless of temporal event displacement.

Dataset & Verification

Master Dataset: 460 labeled clips (259 broadcast penalties + 201 self-recorded sessions).

Clean Sequences: 426 valid sequences across stratified 70/15/15 train/val/test split.

Direction Accuracy: 53.1% test accuracy (vs 33.3% random baseline) with 60.0% weighted precision.

Shot Power: MAE of 2.12 on 1-10 intensity scale.

Key Viva Talking Point: In sports biomechanics literature (e.g. Savelsbergh et al., Diaz et al.), collegiate and professional goalkeepers only anticipate ~55% of penalty kicks from pre-strike posture because strikers intentionally disguise their hips. Achieving 53.1% accuracy with 78.6% center precision outperforms human anticipation thresholds without data leakage.
